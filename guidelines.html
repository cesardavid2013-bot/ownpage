Community guidelines

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Draft pending legal review

Lumi works when people can be themselves without being made to feel small, unsafe or sold to. These are the few rules that make that possible. Breaking them can get your profile removed.

Be who you are

  • Use your own first name and recent photos of yourself. No group shots where nobody can tell which one is you.
  • One account per person. Don't create profiles for someone else, a business or a brand.
  • You must be 18 or older. We remove anyone we believe is younger.

Be decent

  • No harassment, threats, hate or pressure. "No" and silence are answers.
  • No sexual content someone hasn't asked for, in photos, prompts or messages.
  • Nudity and sexual acts are not allowed in profile photos.

Keep it real

  • No asking for money, gift cards, crypto or investments, for any reason.
  • No advertising, links to sell things, or recruiting for other services.
  • No mass messaging or automated accounts.

What we do

A person on our team reads every report. Depending on what happened, we may remove content, ask someone to verify themselves, or remove the account. Repeated or serious violations, including anything involving minors, threats or fraud, lead to a permanent ban and, where required, a report to the authorities.

Verification on Lumi means a moderator matched a live selfie to the profile's photos. It tells you the person looks like their pictures. It is not a background check, and it never means you should skip the usual precautions.

Tell us

On any profile or chat, open the menu and choose Report. The person isn't told who reported them, and reporting also blocks them for you. For anything urgent, contact local emergency services first.
