Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data Lumi ("we") collects when you use the Lumi apps for iOS and Android and the Lumi website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We never sell your personal data.

Data we collect

  • Account data: email address, password (stored only as a salted hash), first name, date of birth, gender and who you want to see.
  • Profile data you choose to add: photos, bio, prompts, job, school, city, height, interests and languages. This is visible to other members.
  • Location: an approximate position (rounded to about 100 m) used to show people near you. Other members only ever see an approximate distance, never your position.
  • Activity: likes, passes, matches, messages, blocks and reports, needed to run the service.
  • Purchases: which plan or pack you bought and its status. Card details are handled by Stripe, Apple or Google; we never see or store them.
  • Technical data: device and log information used for security and to fix problems.

How we use it

  • To create your account and show your profile to compatible members.
  • To deliver matches and messages in real time.
  • To provide paid features you purchase.
  • To keep Lumi safe: detecting fake accounts, spam and abuse, and reviewing reports.
  • To comply with the law.

Sensitive information

Information such as your gender, who you want to meet or content in your profile may reveal sensitive details. You choose what to share, and you can edit or remove it at any time.

Who we share data with

Other members see your public profile. We use service providers for hosting, payments (Stripe, Apple, Google, RevenueCat) and email, bound by contracts to protect your data. We disclose data to authorities only when legally required or to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, photos, prompts and swipes are deleted immediately and your matches are closed; limited records (for example payment records and safety reports) are kept only as long as the law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and CCPA), you can access, correct, export or delete your data and object to certain processing. Most of this can be done in the app under Settings; for anything else, write to support@lumi.app.

Age

Lumi is only for adults aged 18 or over. We delete accounts of anyone we believe is under 18.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@lumi.app.
