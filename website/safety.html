Dating safely

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Most people on Lumi are here for the same reason you are. These habits help keep every date a good one.

Before you meet

  • Chat on Lumi first. Keep the conversation in the app until you feel comfortable.
  • Be wary of anyone who pushes to move off the app quickly, avoids video calls or has a story that doesn't add up.
  • Never send money, gift cards or financial details, whatever the reason.

When you meet

  • Meet in a busy public place, and arrange your own transport.
  • Tell a friend where you are going and who you are meeting.
  • Stay in control of your drinks and your belongings.
  • Leave whenever you want. You don't owe anyone an explanation.

Tools in the app

  • Block: removes the person from your matches and hides you from each other.
  • Report: tells our safety team about fake profiles, harassment, scams or anyone who seems under 18. Reporting also blocks.
  • Incognito (Platinum): only people you like can see you.
  • Hide age and distance (Plus and above).

If you are in danger

Call your local emergency number immediately. Then report the person in the app so we can act.
