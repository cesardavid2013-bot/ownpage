Delete your account

Last updated: 30 September 2026

You can delete your Lumi account at any time. Deletion is permanent.

In the app

  1. Open Lumi and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account, then confirm.

Without the app

Email support@lumi.app from the address linked to your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify that you own the account and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Immediately: your profile, photos, prompts, location, likes and passes. Your matches are closed and you disappear from other members' lists.
  • Kept only where the law requires: payment records (for tax) and safety reports (to prevent banned users from returning), for up to the legally required period.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play. Cancel it in your Apple or Google account settings to stop future charges.
